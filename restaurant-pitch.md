# Restaurant Pitch — c0mpiled-15

A counter pitch for restaurant owners, under a minute, plus the asks and answers to common pushback.

## The pitch (about 45 seconds)

> "Hi, I'm [name]. I'm a student building a free tool for downtown restaurants at the hackathon on campus tonight. Do you have two minutes, or is there a better time?
>
> When your cheese or chicken goes up, most places find out when the monthly numbers come in. By then, some dishes have been losing money for weeks.
>
> With our tool, you upload a photo of your supplier invoice. It reads every line and shows what each dish costs you to make right now, and flags any dish that's losing money.
>
> And if you've got food left near close, you can post a deal to students nearby. It's priced so you never sell below cost, and you set how many.
>
> [Show it on your phone.]
>
> It's free while we pilot it. Could we try it with one of your invoices?"

## The asks, smallest first

Stop at whatever they're comfortable with.

1. **A photo of one invoice.** They can cover account numbers; you only need item names and prices.
2. **Permission to name the restaurant in tonight's demo.** Ask separately whether you can show their real dollar figures, or should round or hide them.
3. **A small deal for tonight**, like a free side with any order after 9pm, capped at a number they pick.
4. **A signature** on a one-page free pilot agreement.

## Common pushback

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "I don't share my invoices." | "Totally fair. Cover anything you like. We won't show your prices publicly unless you say so." |
| "I already know my costs." | "Most owners check monthly. This updates per dish every time a delivery comes in." |
| "I don't want to discount." | "It's only for food you'd throw out anyway, and you set the limit." |
| "I don't have time for another app." | "It's one photo per delivery, in your phone's browser. Nothing to install." |
| "What does it cost?" | "Free for the pilot. We'd only charge later if it's saving you more than it costs." |
| "Are you a real company?" | Be straight: "We started building it today. The pilot means we build it around what you actually need." |

## Delivery tips

- Ask for the owner or manager, and offer to come back if they're slammed. A rushed "no" is worse than a "come back at 6:30."
- Lead with the money-losing dish, not the student deals. Owners care about margins first.
- Write down their exact words. A real owner quote is gold for the video.
